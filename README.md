# Motorcycle-Tail-Bracket
A custom tail bracket for my Kawasaki Vulcan S
I had recently bought a tail bag for my motorcycle and needed a bracket to mount the bag to my bike. Unfortunately a tail bracket costs around $180. So I decided to model my own and have it printed.
In the images folder you can see some test printed I made to make sure the mounting holes lined up before printing the full bracket. 
Once the necessary adjustments were made, I was allowed to use my work's HP 5200 multi-jet fusion 3D printer to print the bracket.
After the bracket was printed, it was dyed black and sprayed with a clear coat to protect it from absorbing water.
Tsunami, which is on the bracket is the name I gave my bike. Some thinks it's lame to name stuff but it's got a back story to it.
